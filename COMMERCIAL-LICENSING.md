# Commercial licensing enquiries — SemiIQ

The V1.0 public research release uses CC BY-NC 4.0 for covered dataset/documentation content and PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 for covered software. These standard licenses include their own specific permitted-use rules. **Commercial authorization is separate and is available only where the licensor(s) hold sufficient rights.**

Kain Lu Low is the release owner and licensing representative, acting on behalf of the authorized licensors for the covered original material.

## When to enquire

If a planned activity requires permissions beyond the applicable standard license, request written permission **before** beginning that activity. Examples that may require review include incorporation of substantial dataset content into a commercial product, paid redistribution of adapted benchmark collections, software use for a purpose outside the PolyForm permitted-use provisions, and licensing proprietary derivatives or services. These are examples for assessment, not automatic legal conclusions about all companies or all AI-training uses.

## Information to provide

- Organization/legal entity and principal contact.
- Dataset, software or both; requested version and component paths.
- Intended purpose, product/service, beneficiaries and whether revenue or commercial advantage is involved.
- Intended acts: internal use, modification, model training/fine-tuning, retrieval, redistribution, API service, publication or sublicensing.
- Access scope, anticipated users, geography, duration and any subcontractors or affiliates.
- Security/confidentiality requirements, third-party source dependencies and proposed attribution.

## Decision pathway

1. The release team confirms the ownership and authority of each relevant rights holder.
2. The parties determine whether the contemplated activity already falls within the public license or a statutory exception.
3. Where additional permission is required and possible, the parties negotiate a **separate signed commercial license**, including covered assets, permitted purposes, modification/redistribution rights, applicable fees, term, attribution, confidentiality, warranties and applicable law.
4. No commercial rights are granted by an enquiry or unsigned proposal.

## Contact

To request commercial licensing review, **open a GitHub issue in this repository entitled “Commercial licensing inquiry”**, briefly identifying the requested component(s). Ask Kain Lu Low, acting on behalf of the authorized licensors, for a private communication channel; **do not post confidential business information, contracts, personal contact details or proposed commercial terms publicly**. The licensing representative can direct the request to the relevant authorized licensor(s).

No pricing, exclusivity, sublicensing, right to model-training outputs, or other commercial right is promised by this document. Publication of these terms does not guarantee any commercial license will be offered.
