# Knowledge taxonomy

Six fixed WP1 modules: `SEM` — Semiconductor Fundamentals; `DEV` — Device Physics; `MAT` — Material Properties; `PRO` — Process Physics; `SIM` — Modeling and Simulation; `ENG` — Engineering Applications.

Subdomains are dynamic, not a globally closed vocabulary. Use the approved WP1 records to obtain actual subdomain IDs/names rather than assuming a fixed subdomain list. WP2 `wp1_link` must agree exactly with its WP1 record ID, module/subdomain identity and knowledge title, ; the public `dataset_release.approval_status` is `Approved` for the collection, while individual approval records are retained privately.
