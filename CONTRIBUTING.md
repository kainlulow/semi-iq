# Contributions

Corrections, new source pointers and benchmark extension proposals are welcome through GitHub issues or pull requests. Include the affected `record_id` or `item_id`, source citation/locator, technical rationale, and affected scoring criteria where relevant.

**Do not directly edit the approved V1.0.0 dataset in place.** Changes to knowledge records or questions must be developed as a new draft, independently reviewed, given human final approval and released under a new version. Keep training prompts or RAG retrieval inputs isolated from WP2 reference answers and scoring rubrics during evaluation.

Contributors should only submit original content or content they have the rights to license. Any accepted contribution requires appropriate license compatibility and authorization under the repository's [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Submission by itself is **not** a rights transfer or a blanket commercial license. Maintainers may request a separate contributor agreement if broader commercial rights are contemplated.

Do not upload copyrighted textbooks, full-text journal articles, proprietary data, private held-out tests, internal audit materials, credentials, personal information, or restricted source archive identifiers.
